## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Entry Trigger (Long)
1. **5+ consecutive bearish candles** — exhaustion sell-off.
2. The **last red candle** has volume ≥ 80% of the average volume of prior red candles (capitulation).
3. A **green candle** forms with its High breaking above the last red candle's High.
4. Entry = break of last red candle's high. SL = lowest low of the entire move. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- 5+ reds = panic exhaustion. Green candle breaking the last red = bulls back in control.
- Volume check is critical — climactic volume, not declining volume.
- BANKNIFTY is ideal for sharper V-shape reversals.
- Rare setup (~50–60 trades / 60 days) — be patient.
