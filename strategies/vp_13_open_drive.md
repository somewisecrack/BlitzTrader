## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Entry Window: **09:18 – 09:45 only**

### Long Entry (Bullish OD)
1. First 3 candles of the day are **all green (bullish)**.
2. At least one is a **Power Candle** (body >75% of range AND range >1.3× avg).
3. Entry = close of 3rd candle. SL = lowest low of all 3 candles. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry (Bearish OD)
1. First 3 candles are **all red (bearish)**, at least one a Power Candle.
2. Entry = close. SL = highest high of all 3 candles. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- OD = institutions committed direction from the first candle with no hesitation.
- **Never use 2m** — too noisy. 5m and 15m are the correct timeframes.
- 1–2 times per week at most. Wait patiently for all 3 conditions.
