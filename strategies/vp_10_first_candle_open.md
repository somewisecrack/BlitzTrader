## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Short Entry
1. Note the **open price of the first 09:15 candle**.
2. After 09:16, a **bearish Power Candle** closes **below that open price**.
3. Entry = close. SL = high of the entry candle.

### Long Entry
1. The first 09:15 candle must itself be **bullish**.
2. After 09:16, a **bullish Power Candle** closes **above the first candle's open**.
3. Entry = close. SL = low of the entry candle.

**Power Candle**: body > 75% of range AND range > 1.3× 5-period avg. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- First candle's open = "line in the sand" for the session direction.
