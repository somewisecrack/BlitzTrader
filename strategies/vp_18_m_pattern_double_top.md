## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Pattern Detection (last 20 bars)
1. Two **swing highs** within 0.5% of each other = Double Top ("M" shape).

### Entry Trigger (Short)
2. Price breaks **below the neckline** (lowest low between the two peaks).
3. Breaking candle must be **bearish**.
4. Entry = close. SL = higher of the two peaks. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- One of the most reliable reversal patterns. Positive PF on ALL 6 tested combinations.
- Especially powerful on BANKNIFTY where double tops often form near R1/supply zones.
- Add Evening Star (VP-15) completing the 2nd top for very high probability.
