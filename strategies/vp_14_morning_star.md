## 🛠️ Setup Rules (3-Candle)
1. **Candle 1**: Large bearish (red) — strong selling.
2. **Candle 2**: Small body/Doji (body ratio < 30%) — indecision.
3. **Candle 3**: Large bullish (green), body > 50% of C1's body, closes above EMA20.
4. Entry = close of C3. SL = lowest low of C1 & C2. Target = 2× risk.
