## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Long Entry
1. In the last 10 bars, find a **bullish candle with lower wick > 2× its body** (master candle).
2. Long wick = buyers rejected lower prices.
3. Current candle is **bullish**, closes **above the master candle's close**.
4. Price must be **above EMA20**.
5. Entry = current close. SL = master candle's low. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry
1. Find a **bearish candle with upper wick > 2× its body** (master candle).
2. Current candle is **bearish**, closes **below the master candle's close**.
3. Price must be **below EMA20**.
4. Entry = current close. SL = master candle's high. Target = 2× risk.
