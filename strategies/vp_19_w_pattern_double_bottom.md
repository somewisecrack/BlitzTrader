## 🛠️ Setup Rules
### Pattern Detection (last 20 bars)
1. Two **swing lows** within 0.5% of each other = Double Bottom ("W" shape).

### Entry Trigger (Long)
2. Price breaks **above the neckline** (highest high between the two troughs).
3. Breaking candle must be **bullish**.
4. Entry = close. SL = lower of the two troughs. Target = 2× risk.
