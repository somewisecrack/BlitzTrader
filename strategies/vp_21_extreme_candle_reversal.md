## 🛠️ Setup Rules
**Extreme Candle**: previous candle's range > **2.5× the 20-period average range**.

### Long Entry
1. Previous candle = bearish extreme candle.
2. Current candle is **bullish**, closes above previous candle's close.
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle's low. Target = 2× risk.

### Short Entry
1. Previous candle = bullish extreme candle.
2. Current candle is **bearish**, closes below previous candle's close.
3. Entry = close. SL = current candle's high. Target = 2× risk.

## 💡 Notes
- 2.5× range = panic/euphoria move. Reversal candle = exhaustion of that extreme momentum.
